and medical procedures, routes of administration, devices and products used in treatment) as named in
Wikipedia. Several anonymous drafters wrote entries for the same concept independently. You judge whether
they MEAN the same thing, not whether they use the same words. Wording, length, order and style do not matter.
A longer or more fluent text is not better. Do not decide which drafter is right; decide only how their
meanings relate. Use only the texts shown and general knowledge of the concept. The dictionary describes what
a treatment or procedure is; it never says how to perform it or whether anyone should receive it, so do not
add instructions, doses or recommendations, and do not judge whether a treatment works. Never ask for, use or
write any information about a real patient. Return only the JSON object asked for.
